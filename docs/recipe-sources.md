# Recipe sources

[Shared Recipes](https://smart7-offline.vercel.app/#library) reads open recipe issues from the [GitHub catalog](https://github.com/OneTop4458/HARIO-Smart7-BT/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Arecipe). Each recipe issue carries the executable program, its source, and any translated text. Copy a recipe to My Recipes to keep it in the browser.

## Original Smart7 recipes

The 19 `recipe-archive` issues preserve the original HARIO Smart7 shared recipes whose brew settings could be read and validated. They include Tetsu Kasuya's hot and iced 4:6 recipes, HARIO's four taste adjustments for Auto Mode, and original community submissions. [Archive issues](https://github.com/OneTop4458/HARIO-Smart7-BT/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3Arecipe-archive) · [HARIO taste adjustment guide](https://smart7.jp/tips.html)

The Japanese and Korean search forms survive in Wayback snapshots. They submitted to separate result pages, and the checked Wayback index held no captures of those results. The original detail-page index returned No. 1061, the iced 4:6 recipe already in the catalog. The 2018 Smart7 campaign and recipe No. 1064 are mentioned in contemporary sources, which do not supply the complete programs. A 360 mL Smart7 BT recipe in a user review shows the first four settings but leaves the last two off screen. [Archived search form](https://web.archive.org/web/20191210055115/https://smart7.jp/refine_search.php) · [Archived No. 1061](https://web.archive.org/web/20240226122051/https://smart7.jp/detail.php?recipe_no=1061) · [HARIO campaign announcement](https://harioofficial.seesaa.net/article/459583730.html) · [No. 1064 reference](https://bbs.kakaku.com/bbs/K0000875962/SortID%3D21630906/) · [Smart7 BT user review](https://coffee-pharmacist.com/hario-smart7#toc7)

## V60 recipes adapted for Smart7

HARIO still publishes many V60 recipes and brewing guides. These are generally hand-pour recipes. The eight programs below adapt the published settings for the Smart7 BT's fixed shower, 10 mL volume increments, 270 mL minimum, and 96 °C maximum. Each issue explains the changes and contains Korean, English, and Japanese descriptions. The programs pass the web app's validator. They remain untested on a physical Smart7 BT.
